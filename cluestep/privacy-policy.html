ClueStep ("this service") is a static web puzzle game based on row and column clues. It does not offer account registration, login, payments, user-posted content, or server-side game saving. This privacy policy explains how information is handled.

1. Information collected

  • Language preference: Your language choice (Korean/English/Japanese) is stored in your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your browser and is not sent to any server.
  • Game progress: The game runs entirely in your browser; puzzle progress and results are not saved on any server.
  • Account data: There are no sign-up or login features, so no account information is collected.

2. Processing by hosting and network providers

This service is delivered as static files. During web hosting and transmission, the hosting provider and network (transmission) provider may process access logs (such as IP addresses and access times). Such information is managed under each provider's policies and is not separately collected or used by this service.

3. Advertising and analytics

This service uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure page visits, page views, and basic performance metrics. No advertising SDKs are currently enabled, and custom events such as game starts or puzzle completions are not yet collected. Cloudflare may act as the analytics provider; please refer to Cloudflare's policies (https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details on how it processes data. If advertising or additional analytics tools are introduced in the future, this privacy policy will be updated and the changes announced in advance.

4. Cookies and local storage

This service does not use tracking cookies. The only item stored in localStorage is your language preference, and you can remove it at any time through your browser settings.

5. Third-party disclosure

This service does not sell or provide your personal information to third parties, except for access-log processing by hosting/network providers as described in Section 2.

6. Privacy inquiries

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact dfd365.contact@gmail.com.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy takes effect on 2026-09-11 and will be updated and announced if features change (for example, if advertising or analytics are introduced).
